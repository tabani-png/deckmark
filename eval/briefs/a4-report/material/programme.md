# Kestrel Valley Water Authority: Mains Renewal Programme review material (FICTIONAL public body, evaluation material only)

Kestrel Valley Water Authority (fictional) is renewing old water mains to cut leakage. The audit committee asked for a value-for-money review of the Mains Renewal Programme at the end of year 3 of 5.

## Programme facts (GBP millions unless stated; finance ledger to 30 September 2026)
- Approved budget: 186.0.
- Spend to date: 121.4, of which Lot A (northern contractor) 68.3 and Lot B (southern contractor) 53.1.
- Mains renewed to date: 412 km of 640 km planned.
- Planned cost per km: 0.29.
- Leakage: 142 megalitres a day at the start of the programme, 118 megalitres a day now; target 105 by March 2028.
- Lot A renewed 236 km; Lot B renewed 176 km.
- Customer interruptions over 12 hours: 31 in year 1, 19 in year 2, 12 in year 3.

## Findings from the review team
1. Delivery is behind plan: 64% of the planned length is done at 60% of the time elapsed, but 65% of the budget is spent.
2. Lot B unit costs run above Lot A; the contract has no unit-rate benchmarking clause.
3. Leakage reduction is on track for the 2028 target only if the remaining 228 km are delivered on time.
4. The programme board has no independent member.

## Recommendations proposed
1. Add unit-rate benchmarking to the Lot B contract at the next variation.
2. Appoint an independent member to the programme board.
3. Report cost per km and leakage quarterly to the audit committee.

## Format
An A4 PDF report for the audit committee papers. Reader: the audit committee. Decision: accept the recommendations.
