Situation

Close takes the largest share of time

Close and reconciliation take 34% of the time in a 210-person finance team, the largest share. Month-end close runs 11 business days against a peer median of 6.

Complication

Manual work is slowing the close

The team posts 4,800 manual journal entries a month. The bank had 3 regulatory restatements in 2024.

Resolution

Start with reconciliation and AP coding

These two use cases hold 35,000 of the 58,000 hours a year that clients estimate AI could save. Fund three waves within $4.0M over 18 months.
