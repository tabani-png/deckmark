Lakeshore Mutual: deploy AI in finance in three waves over 18 months

The decision: approve the phased roadmap and the $4.0M budget.

November 2026 · Draft for discussionConfidential

Start AI in finance with reconciliation and report validation, then scale in three waves

Situation

Finance has 210 FTE. 34% of their time goes to close and reconciliation, the largest share of any activity.

Complication

Month-end close takes 11 business days against a peer median of 6. Finance makes 4,800 manual journal entries a month, and there were 3 restatements in 2024.

Resolution

Five use cases save an estimated 58,000 hours a year (client estimates). Wave one, reconciliation matching and regulatory report validation, carries 27,000 of them. We ask the committee to approve the roadmap and the $4.0M budget over 18 months.

Hours are client estimates and have not been validated. Their dollar value is not yet sized (page 10).

Source: Lakeshore internal time survey; client-commissioned close benchmark; client use-case estimates; team analysis.
