The ask

$85.0M senior secured unitranche

Refinances the $60.0M existing term loan, funds the $22.0M Ridgeway Box add-on and pays $3.0M of fees. Five years at S+575, 1.00% floor, 98.0 OID.

Leverage

2.96x on pro forma EBITDA

Gross debt of $85.0M over FY24 pro forma EBITDA of $28.7M. On reported FY24 EBITDA alone it is 3.32x.

Cushion

37.6% EBITDA fall to the opening covenant

Measured on gross debt, with no cash netted. The 4.25x step-down leaves a 30.3% fall. The downside case is not in the material.

Gates

Five conditions must close first

The QoE, the plant 3 environmental report, FY25 year-to-date actuals, debt service and downside case, and the equity reconciliation. Organic FY25 budget EBITDA of $25.3M is below FY24's $25.6M.
