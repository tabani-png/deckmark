## M&A Pitch Book

The bank's deck for winning a mandate or advising on strategic options. Credentials, the read on the situation, valuation, who would buy, and how the process would run.

**Reader and decision.** A founder, CEO, CFO or board considering a sale, acquisition or capital raise; the MD presents it in the room. It drives **hire**: mandate this adviser, at this valuation view, with this process.

**Length.** 20 to 40 slides plus appendix. A first-meeting book can be 12.

**Short form (a discussion document or thin material, 8 to 12 slides).** When there is no full valuation work: cover, situation and the question, the read on the company in kpi-strip plus one chart, the options as a two-column-compare or harvey-table with a stated basis, indicative valuation only if the material carries the multiples, the buyer or partner universe as a ranked list, next steps. Credentials only if the user supplies them.

**Order.**
1. **cover**: client name (if the pitch is to them) or codename, "Discussion materials", date, "Preliminary draft, subject to change".
2. **disclaimer**.
3. **exec-summary-scr**: why now, the valuation range headline, the recommended path, why us.
4. **Credentials**: tombstone-grid of relevant deals (only those the user supplies) and a ranked-bar of league table position if given, with source and period.
5. **Situation overview, 2-4 slides**: company snapshot kpi-strip, financial performance chart-takeaway, share price vs index (if public), strategic issues.
6. **Market and M&A context, 2-3 slides**: sector growth, consolidation, recent transactions with multiples, buyer appetite.
7. **Strategic alternatives**: harvey-table comparing status quo, sale to strategic, sale to sponsor, minority, IPO, recap.
8. **Valuation, 4-7 slides**: comps-table (trading), comps-table (precedents), DCF summary with sensitivity-table (WACC vs terminal growth or exit multiple), LBO ability-to-pay, then **football-field**.
9. **Buyer universe, 2-3 slides**: strategic and financial buyers tiered, each with rationale and ability to pay; matrix-2x2 of strategic fit vs capacity.
10. **Process**: process-chevrons (preparation, first round, second round, signing), timeline-gantt in weeks.
11. **Team**: org-chart of the deal team by role.
12. **Appendix**: comps detail, DCF build, WACC.

**Lean form (default).** Exec summary as a KPI row over 3-5 one-line claims; situation as chart + takeaway-bar; strategic alternatives as a harvey-table with plain cells, never paragraphs; process as a `flow` plus the gantt; credentials as tombstones. Valuation pages are tables and the football-field: numbers carry them.

**What practitioners expect that a model forgets.**
- The football field is the signature page: each bar names the multiple range used and the metric (LTM or NTM EBITDA); current EV or offer price as a reference line.
- Comps have an as-of date and say whether multiples are LTM or NTM; mean and median rows; the subject row set apart.
- Precedents carry announcement date, acquirer and target, EV and the multiple; stale deals are flagged.
- DCF shows WACC, terminal method and growth, and the share of value in the terminal.
- "Preliminary draft, subject to change" and "for discussion purposes only" appear on every page footer.
- LBO ability-to-pay and any financing page show debt at net proceeds after OID and fees; sources and uses never tie at face value.
- Buyer rationale is specific (adjacent product, geography, portfolio company), not "strategic fit".

**Honesty rules.** No tombstones, league table ranks or multiples that the user did not provide. If comps data is missing, the comps table and the football field are not drawn: one `DATA NEEDED: FactSet/CapIQ pull as of <date>` marker stands where the table would go and the pull is listed on the open-items page (a table of gap cells trips decklint's gap-sprawl). Buyer names come from the user or are left as tier placeholders ("Strategic A: adjacent software platform").

**How it ends.** Proposed next steps and timetable to launch, then the team page, then appendix. Not a "why us" slogan page.

**Density.** Dense in valuation and comps (9 to 11px figures allowed in tables, never below the theme minimum); medium elsewhere.

**Words.** 16:9 target 40-60, cap 80 · A4 cap 300 · no prose tables. Body words per content slide (decklint `wordy`, `prose-table`). The MD presents it: the slide holds the exhibit, the banker says the rest; show it, do not say it: one exhibit carries the slide, the takeaway is one line, detail goes to the appendix or the speaker notes.
