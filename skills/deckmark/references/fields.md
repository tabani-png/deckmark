# Fields

Who reads the deck and how they read it. The field sets vocabulary, the numbers that must be found in the first ten seconds, the formats readers expect, and the default storyline and density. It also lists the tells that make a reader in that field stop trusting the deck. Each field also has a **Look**: the house voice its readers trust (what the slides are built from, how colour is used) and what it refuses. Pick the field at Step 2 from the prompt; when two apply (a credit fund's LP letter), the reader's field wins over the writer's.

---

## pe (private equity)

**Vocabulary.** Entry and exit multiple, EV, equity cheque, rollover, MIP, LBO, IRR, MOIC, value creation bridge, add-on / buy-and-build, QoE, 100-day plan, platform, hold period, dry powder, sponsor.
**Numbers first.** Adjusted EBITDA and its add-backs; entry multiple; leverage at close; base-case IRR and MOIC; how much of the return comes from multiple expansion.
**Formats.** IC memo (memo or deck), LBO returns pages, sensitivity grids (entry vs exit multiple), value creation waterfall, rag diligence tracker. USD m to one decimal, multiples as "8.5x", returns as "2.4x / 22%".
**Defaults.** Storyline `ic-memo`; for fundraising `fund-pitch`; for portfolio reporting `lp-letter`. Density dense.
**Look.** Dense and exact: tables of numbers at 10 to 11px, a returns bridge, a sensitivity grid with the base case outlined. One highlight colour for the deal's own row or bar. Titles carry the number (*Base case returns 2.4x / 22% at 9.5x entry*). Refused: hero figures with no case named, gradients, a value creation story told in prose.
**Distrust tells.** Returns driven mainly by multiple expansion with no comment; a downside case that still returns 2x; add-backs in one line; no stated diligence basis; adjusted EBITDA with no reported figure beside it; "proprietary deal flow" with no number.

## credit (private credit, direct lending, leveraged finance)

**Vocabulary.** Senior secured, unitranche, first lien / second lien, margin over SOFR or SONIA, floor, OID, call protection, PIK, covenant-lite, maintenance covenant, headroom, step-downs, EBITDA cushion, LTV, DSCR, FCCR, recovery, hold size, sponsor support.
**Numbers first.** Total and senior net leverage; interest cover and FCCR; LTV; covenant headroom in the downside; EBITDA decline to breach and to default; FCF after debt service.
**Formats.** Credit memo, covenant table, cap table with cumulative leverage, downside case table, surveillance dashboard. Leverage as "4.8x", pricing as "S+575, 0.75% floor, 98 OID".
**Defaults.** Storyline `credit-memo`; monitoring as `board-pack` turned lender update with dashboard-tiles. Density dense.
**Look.** Ledger-like: covenant and cap tables set tight, leverage as a ladder, headroom in base and downside side by side. Status colour only with a stated threshold; the downside case is never lighter than the base. Refused: a single base-case number, optimistic colour on headroom, rounded leverage.
**Distrust tells.** Headroom only shown in the base case; leverage with no statement of which EBITDA; uncapped add-backs unflagged; covenant definitions missing; sources and uses that do not tie; a recovery analysis that assumes the sponsor's exit multiple.

## banking (investment banking, M&A, ECM/DCM)

**Vocabulary.** Mandate, sell-side / buy-side, teaser, CIM, IOI, LOI, process letter, data room, comps, precedents, DCF, LBO floor, football field, control premium, synergies, accretion/dilution, fairness opinion, tombstone.
**Numbers first.** LTM and NTM EBITDA; the valuation range and the multiples behind it; the buyer universe and who can pay most; timetable.
**Formats.** Pitch book, CIM, teaser, process letter; dense comps tables at small sizes; "Preliminary draft, subject to change" footers; codenames. Figures in USD m, multiples to one decimal, dates as "Q3 2026".
**Defaults.** Storylines `pitch-book`, `cim`, `teaser`. Density dense in valuation, medium elsewhere.
**Look.** The book: a codename on the cover, "Preliminary draft" in the furniture, dense comps at small sizes, a football field with its multiples, one deep field colour for table heads. Refused: logos not supplied, buyer names guessed, a teaser that identifies the company.
**Distrust tells.** Comps with no as-of date or mixed LTM/NTM; a football field with no stated multiples; a teaser that identifies the company; buyer rationale that says "strategic fit"; tombstones that cannot be confirmed.

## asset-mgmt (asset management, hedge funds, fund of funds, allocators)

**Vocabulary.** AUM, NAV, alpha, tracking error, information ratio, Sharpe, drawdown, attribution, benchmark, share class, net of fees, PME, quartile, vintage, TVPI, DPI, RVPI, capital call, DDQ, GIPS.
**Numbers first.** Net returns vs benchmark over standard periods; drawdown; fees; for private funds DPI and TVPI by vintage; team stability.
**Formats.** Fund pitch deck, factsheet, quarterly letter, attribution tables, performance tables with 1/3/5/10/SI columns. Returns to one decimal %, always labelled gross or net, always with as-of date.
**Defaults.** Storylines `fund-pitch`, `lp-letter`. Density medium.
**Look.** Calm and editorial: one big number a page (net return, DPI), performance tables with standard periods, an as-of date in every unit line, generous space around a single chart. Refused: gross returns alone, cherry-picked periods, a wall of KPIs.
**Distrust tells.** Gross returns shown alone; periods cherry-picked (no standard periods); no losses in a track record; quartile claims with no source; returns from a prior firm with no attribution; changing benchmark.

## consulting (strategy, operations, transformation)

**Vocabulary.** Governing thought, SCR, key line, MECE, issue tree, hypothesis, size of the prize, levers, run-rate, quick wins, wave plan, PMO, steerco.
**Numbers first.** Size of the prize (EBITDA or cost, as a range), the gap to peers, the cost of doing nothing, investment and payback.
**Formats.** Action-titled landscape slides, unit line under the title, source line bottom-left, tracker in the corner, chart-plus-sidebar, harvey tables, 2x2s, waterfalls, exhibit numbering in reports.
**Defaults.** Storyline `scr-strategy`. Density medium to dense.
**Look.** Answer-first slides: an action title, a unit line, one exhibit with a sidebar of three numbered points, a tracker in the corner, exhibit numbers. Restrained palette, one accent for the bar the title names. Refused: topic titles, icon rows, two-by-twos whose axes are opinions.
**Distrust tells.** Topic titles ("Market overview"); titles that read as an argument while the bodies prove something else; levers that do not sum to the bridge; benchmarks with no source; a 2x2 whose axes are opinions; recommendations with no owner or date.

## cfo / board (corporate finance, FP&A, board reporting)

**Vocabulary.** Budget, reforecast, outturn, run-rate, variance (favourable/adverse), price-volume-mix, bridge, cash conversion, working capital days, liquidity headroom, covenant compliance, capex, ROIC, KPI scorecard, decisions required.
**Numbers first.** Actual vs budget vs prior year for revenue, EBITDA and cash; full-year forecast vs budget; liquidity headroom.
**Formats.** Board pack, monthly flash, investor-day financial section; variance columns with consistent sign convention; RAG with stated thresholds; appendix statements.
**Defaults.** Storylines `board-pack`, `investor-day`. Density medium.
**Look.** A scorecard that reads in a minute: actual, budget and prior year in fixed columns, variances signed by effect, RAG with the threshold in a footnote, decisions on page 2. Refused: decisions at the back, colour by sign rather than effect, a KPI set that changes month to month.
**Distrust tells.** Decisions buried at the back; variances described not explained; a KPI set that changes each quarter; EBITDA with no cash view; colour on variances that follows the number's sign, not its effect on profit; bad news first appearing deep in the pack.

## family-office / wealth

**Vocabulary.** Investment policy statement, strategic asset allocation, ranges, rebalancing, liquidity tiers, capital calls, look-through exposure, concentrated position, tax lots, custodian, total cost, mandate, next generation, trustees.
**Numbers first.** Total wealth and change; net return vs policy benchmark; liquidity available and calls due; total cost in money.
**Formats.** Client review report, allocation vs policy charts, performance tables by asset class, private-markets commitment table, glossary. Plain language on summary pages.
**Defaults.** Storyline `wealth-review`; for a family office investing directly, `ic-memo` in a lighter form. Density sparse to medium on summaries, dense in appendix.
**Look.** Plain and warm: summary pages in plain language with one figure each, allocation against policy as a bar with ranges, costs in money, a glossary at the back. Refused: jargon on the first page, basis-point-only fees, product names as headings.
**Distrust tells.** Fees shown only in basis points or not at all; returns without benchmark; private fund IRRs for young funds presented as meaningful; jargon on the first page; product pushing; holdings that do not reconcile to the custodian.

## public-sector

**Vocabulary.** Value for money (economy, efficiency, effectiveness), outturn, forecast at completion, business case, benefits realisation, accounting officer, spending review, price base (nominal/real), outcomes, accountability, recommendations.
**Numbers first.** Budget and forecast cost at completion; spend to date; delay against plan; outcomes vs target; people affected.
**Formats.** Report with numbered paragraphs, key facts page, figures numbered with sources, recommendations table addressed to named bodies; briefing slides that are sparse.
**Defaults.** Storyline `public-sector-review`. Density medium, text-led.
**Look.** A report: numbered paragraphs, a key facts page, figures numbered with sources and price base, recommendations addressed to named bodies in a table. Neutral palette, no promotional tone. Refused: persuasive headlines, unverified programme claims, individuals named.
**Distrust tells.** Figures with no price base or source; benefits taken from the programme's own claims unverified; findings, conclusions and recommendations blurred together; consultancy jargon; persuasive or promotional tone; individuals named.
